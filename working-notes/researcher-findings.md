## Key Facts

### Q1: What exactly applies from 2 August 2026?

**Article 50 Transparency Obligations (BINDING, 2 AUGUST 2026):**
- Article 50(1): Providers must ensure direct interactions with humans are disclosed as AI [Source: EUR-Lex, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj]
- Article 50(3): Deployers of emotion recognition and biometric categorisation systems must disclose to exposed persons [Source: EUR-Lex]
- Article 50(4): Deployers of systems generating/manipulating image, audio, video or public-interest text deepfakes must disclose artificial origin [Source: EUR-Lex]
- **Grace period exception:** Article 50(2) watermarking/detectability for existing systems extends to 2 Dec 2026; new systems must comply immediately on 2 Aug 2026 [Source: Digital Omnibus adoption notice, European Commission]

**Member State Penalty Regimes & Enforcement (BINDING, 2 AUGUST 2026):**
- General enforcement begins 2 August 2026 [Source: EUR-Lex]
- AI Office enforcement powers for GPAI models activate 2 August 2026 [Source: European Commission, AI Office documentation]

**Annex III High-Risk System Obligations (DELAYED TO 2 DECEMBER 2027):**
- Originally scheduled 2 August 2026, now postponed by Digital Omnibus to 2 Dec 2027 [Source: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, published 24 July 2026, entered into force 27 July 2026]
- Applies to high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III (employment, education, critical infrastructure, law enforcement, migration, justice) [Source: EUR-Lex, Digital Omnibus confirmation]

**Annex I High-Risk Systems Embedded in Regulated Products (DELAYED TO 2 AUGUST 2028):**
- Medical devices, machinery, lifts containing high-risk AI delayed from 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Aug 2028 [Source: Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744]

**National AI Regulatory Sandboxes (DELAYED TO 2 AUGUST 2027):**
- Member State requirement originally 2 Aug 2026, now 2 Aug 2027 [Source: Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744]

**New Prohibitions (BINDING, 2 DECEMBER 2026):**
- Two new prohibitions covering non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material take effect 2 Dec 2026 [Source: Digital Omnibus]

## Background Context

The EU AI Act, adopted by the European Parliament on 13 March 2024 and approved by the Council on 21 May 2024, represents the world's first comprehensive horizontal legal framework for artificial intelligence. It entered into force on 1 August 2024 and is being implemented in phases:

- **2 February 2025:** Prohibitions on manipulative AI and social scoring; AI literacy obligations
- **2 August 2025:** Rules for general-purpose AI models; national competent authority designation
- **2 August 2026:** Originally scheduled for major tranche (Annex III high-risk, Article 50 transparency, penalty regimes, regulatory sandboxes)
- **2 December 2026:** New prohibitions (intimate imagery, child sexual abuse material)

The European Commission proposed the Digital Omnibus on 19 November 2025 to address three implementation gaps: (1) incompleteness of CEN-CENELEC harmonised standards, (2) lack of designated and resourced national competent authorities in all member states, (3) absence of operational conformity assessment infrastructure.

After trilogue negotiations, the Digital Omnibus was endorsed by the European Parliament on 16 June 2026 and approved by the Council on 29 June 2026. It entered into force on 27 July 2026—three days before the original 2 August 2026 milestone.

**The central story tension:** The regulation's largest tranche of obligations is simultaneously being postponed by the very institution that drafted it, while a smaller but significant set of transparency and enforcement provisions proceed on schedule. For non-EU vendors (especially in Taiwan and Greater China), this creates dual compliance calendars: immediate transparency/GPAI rules on 2 August 2026, but high-risk obligations deferred to December 2027 and August 2028.
