## Primary Sources (Official / Authoritative)

### 1. EUR-Lex: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — EU AI Act

- **Full Title:** REGULATION (EU) 2024/1689 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 13 March 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence
- **URL:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- **Publication Date:** 12 July 2024 (Official Journal)
- **Language:** English (Official EU text)
- **Credibility:** PRIMARY — Foundational legal text; all article numbers, fine ceilings, Annex classifications derive from this
- **Key Sections for This Cycle:**
  - Article 5: Prohibited AI practices (tied to €35M / 7% fine tier)
  - Articles 6–49: High-risk system requirements (tied to €15M / 3% fine tier)
  - Article 50: Transparency obligations (chatbots, synthetic content, emotion recognition)
  - Article 99: Administrative fines structure (€35M / 7%, €15M / 3%, €7.5M / 1.5%)
  - Annex III: High-risk AI system use cases (employment, education, critical infrastructure, law enforcement, migration, justice, community services)

### 2. Digital Omnibus on AI: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744

- **Full Title:** REGULATION (EU) 2026/1744 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 29 June 2026 amending Regulations (EU) 2019/1148, (EU) 2019/1938, (EU) 2020/1503, (EU) 2020/1828, (EU) 2021/696, (EU) 2021/2113, (EU) 2022/1925, (EU) 2023/1670 and (EU) 2024/1689
- **Official Journal Publication:** 24 July 2026
- **Entry into Force:** 27 July 2026 (3 days after publication)
- **Language:** English (Official EU text)
- **Credibility:** PRIMARY — The definitive amendment establishing the postponements and grace periods
- **Key Changes for This Cycle:**
  - Annex III high-risk systems: 2 August 2026 → 2 December 2027
  - Annex I embedded high-risk systems: 2 August 2026 → 2 August 2028
  - National regulatory sandboxes: 2 August 2026 → 2 August 2027
  - Article 50(2) watermarking grace period for existing systems: 2 December 2026
  - New prohibitions (intimate imagery, CSAM): 2 December 2026

## Regulatory Analysis

### 10. Bright Defense: "Pragmatic admission that infrastructure does not exist"

- **URL:** https://brightdefense.com/
- **Publication Date:** 29 July 2026
- **Language:** English
- **Source Type:** Regulatory analysis / consultancy
- **Credibility:** SECONDARY — Candid assessment of why the Omnibus was necessary
- **Key Quote:** "On July 24, 2026, the EU published Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI, which entered into force on July 27. It is a pragmatic admission that the infrastructure for enforcement simply does not exist. The regulation effectively resets the clock on the most demanding requirements of the AI Act. Annex III high-risk system obligations, originally slated for August 2, 2026, are now deferred to December 2, 2027—a 16-month reprieve."
- **Use:** Explains the underlying rationale: infrastructure gaps (standards, national authorities, conformity assessment bodies) justified the deferral. Emphasizes realism over ideology in regulation.
